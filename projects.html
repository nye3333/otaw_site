Projects

Below are three interactive tools used in my geometric group theory work: finite-depth balls of free groups in 3D, Stallings folding on labelled roses, and line-pattern pictures for cyclic subgroups of F2.

Infinite Free-Group Cayley Graphs

Explore finite-depth balls of F2 or F3 as forced-directed graphs; tune depth and link visibility from the on-canvas controls.

Whitehead Folding

Enter one reduced word per petal for ranks 2–5 and step through deterministic Stallings folds to quotient graphs (standard rose when immersion succeeds).

Fold log

    Line Patterns in F2

    Truncate the Cayley tree for ⟨a,b⟩ at chosen depth, shade cosets of ⟨w⟩, inspect local Whitehead graphs at vertices, and optionally splice along intervals between two vertices.
